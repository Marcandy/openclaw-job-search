---
name: job-batch-operations
description: Inspect, monitor and reconcile an authorized OpenClaw application batch, including expiry, daily capacity and held deliveries.
---

Use the explicitly selected runtime and authoritative SQLite store. Check active workers before launching or changing inputs. Never create a second ledger or bypass a held worker by copying the database.

Keep requested additional applications, this run's confirmed receipts, held deliveries and remaining capacity separate from daily totals. Read `batchProgress`, `current`, `stopReason` and durable outcomes; a scheduler job or a successful command is not employer acceptance.

A dated grant ends at local midnight. Check available time before starting a form and before every final click, including the click after applicant-entered email verification. Stop on expiry; renew a continuation deliberately under the user's existing scope. Do not reset the requested count at midnight.

Waits for applicant-entered codes must show the company, role and deadline. Never read a mailbox to solve the gate. Preserve submitted and uncertain destination claims; reconcile uncertainty only from actual employer evidence.

Preflight failure memory expires or invalidates when the posting, adapter or confirmed facts change. Form-only changes are discovered on the next bounded probe. That does not authorize retrying a submitted or uncertain destination. Missing personal facts and legal consent remain unknown.

Before deploying code, run the regression suite and inspect the source/config/policy identity. Renew pinned inputs through the documented deployment procedure and verify scheduler readback. A skill file alone is not installed application logic. Do not start applications merely to validate a deployment.
